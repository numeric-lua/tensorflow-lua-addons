# Changelog

## 0.4.0

- contrastive loss.

## 0.3.0

- triplet loss;
- weight-normalization helper.

## 0.2.0

- Cohen's kappa metric;
- NovoGrad optimizer;
- CSVLogger callback.

## 0.1.0

- gelu, swish, mish, lisht, and tanhshrink activations;
- focal, dice, and tversky losses;
- F1, F-beta, Matthews correlation, R², and Pearson metrics;
- group, instance, and layer normalization;
- LAMB optimizer;
- TimeStopping, TerminateOnNaN, and BackupAndRestore callbacks;
- dependency-free test suite and Lua 5.1 CI.
